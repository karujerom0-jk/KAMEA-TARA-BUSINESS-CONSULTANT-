Our proposal approach

A clear plan starts with a clear project

We work with you to organise the project information and prepare a professional document suited to the intended audience or funding opportunity.

  • Project background and identified need
  • Objectives, activities and implementation plan
  • Beneficiaries and expected outcomes
  • Budget information supplied or confirmed by the client
  • Supporting documents and submission checklist
