What We Do

Practical services for business growth

From getting your business registered to preparing a professional proposal, we provide support at important stages of your business and development journey.

▣

SME Business Registration

Support for individuals and small businesses establishing a recognised business presence.

  • IPA business name registration assistance
  • IRC Tax Identification Number (TIN) registration assistance
  • Business profile preparation
  • Bank account opening documentation support
▤

Company Registration

Guidance and documentation support for entrepreneurs setting up a company.

  • IPA company registration assistance
  • IRC TIN registration assistance
  • Company profile preparation
  • Bank account opening documentation support
♧

Association Registration (INC)

Support for associations and community groups working towards shared goals.

  • IPA association registration assistance
  • IRC registration assistance
  • Newspaper advertisement coordination
  • Profile and bank documentation support
  • Indicative processing duration: 4 weeks
